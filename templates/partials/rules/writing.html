{# How Writing points are won: shown under the Writing target on the Writing and Tracking pages. writing.py and database.mark_writing_piece are the rule. #}

AI marks each piece like a teacher, on four criteria worth 20 points each, so 80 in all; for IELTS tasks these are the official criteria, with a band estimate. Your score is your points.

One diary a day earns points, and each IELTS question earns them once. Revising a piece after feedback is practice: you see the new score, but it earns nothing.

The target is weekly, Monday to Sunday. Points a week falls short of are added to the next week's target, until you make them up.
