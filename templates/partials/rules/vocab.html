{# How Vocab points are won and lost: shown under the Vocab goal on the Practice and Tracking pages. practice.py's sentence_points() is the rule. #}

Each sentence is worth points by how hard it is: the practice type (Fill the blank 1, Fix the mistake 2, Translate 3, Use it 3), plus 1 for every 4 words and 1 for each word of 7 letters or more, up to 20.

Right: you win them all. Almost: you lose a third. Wrong or Show answer: you lose half. Always rounded down, so points stay whole.

The target is weekly, Monday to Sunday. Points a week falls short of are added to the next week's target, until you make them up.
