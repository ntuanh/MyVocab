{# How Listening points are won and lost: shown under the Listening target on the Listening and Tracking pages. listening.py's score_points() is the rule. #}

Do the episode's BBC quiz or worksheet, then enter how many you got right. Each right answer wins 4 points and each wrong one loses 2, so 5 out of 6 is +18 and a guess is worth nothing on average.

Only your first score for an episode counts. Later scores are kept as practice, so retaking a quiz you already know earns nothing.

The target is weekly, Monday to Sunday. Points a week falls short of are added to the next week's target, until you make them up.
