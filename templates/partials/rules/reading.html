{# How Reading points are won and lost: shown under the Reading target on the Reading and Tracking pages. reading.py's submit() is the rule. #}

Each part is an IELTS-style Academic Reading passage with 13 questions. Each right answer wins 3 points, each wrong one loses 1, and a blank costs nothing, so 10 right and 3 wrong is +27.

Only your first try at a part counts. Later tries are kept as practice, so redoing a part you have already seen earns nothing.

The plan is one part a day. The target is weekly, Monday to Sunday, and points a week falls short of are added to the next week's target.
