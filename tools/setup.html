    🔑 Your free keys

    MyVocab uses two free services. Get a key from each, then paste it here. No card or payment is needed.

    Gemini key

    Needed

    For meanings, examples, Vietnamese translations and marking your writing.

    Get a Gemini key ↗
    1. Sign in with your Google account.
    2. Click Create API key, then the copy button next to it.
    3. Come back here and paste it below.

    Pexels key

    Optional

    For a picture next to each word.

    Get a Pexels key ↗
    1. Click Get Started and make a free account.
    2. Fill in the short form (for the website, write personal English study).
    3. Copy Your API Key and paste it below.

    Password for “My Words”

    Needed

    Choose any password. It keeps your word list page private. Write it down somewhere.

    🎉 All set!

    MyVocab is ready. From now on:

    1. Double-click the MyVocab icon on your desktop (it is in the Start menu too).
    2. A black window opens, then MyVocab opens in your browser. Keep that black window open while you study.
    3. When you finish, close the black window.
